A Digital Loyalty System is small to build, but extremely powerful for Cambodian SMEs.

Let me break it down clearly and practically.

🎯 Product Name Idea

“Loyalkh” – Digital Loyalty for Local Businesses

🚨 The Real Problem (In Cambodia)

Small businesses:

Use paper stamp cards (easy to lose, fake, or damage)

Have no customer database

Don’t know who their repeat customers are

Can’t reward loyal customers properly

Rely only on Facebook posts for retention

Customers:

Forget about the shop

Lose physical loyalty cards

Switch to competitors easily

💡 The Solution: Digital Stamp System

Instead of paper cards:

👉 Every customer gets a digital loyalty account
👉 Every visit = 1 point / 1 stamp
👉 After X visits → reward unlocked

Simple. Powerful. Affordable.

🌐 Public Website (Customer Side)

This is what customers see.

1️⃣ Customer Login (Phone Number Based)

Enter phone number

Receive OTP (optional for later)

Access loyalty account

2️⃣ Loyalty Dashboard

Shows:

Current points

Reward progress bar

Reward history

Expiration date (optional)

Example:

☕ Coffee World
Points: 7 / 10
🎁 Reward: Free Large Coffee

3️⃣ QR Code Check-in

Each customer has a unique QR code.

At the shop:

Staff scans customer QR
OR

Customer scans shop QR

System automatically adds points.

Very simple.

🖥 Admin Portal (Business Side)

This is where the magic happens.

📊 1. Dashboard

Shows:

Total customers

Active customers

Today’s visits

Top repeat customers

This gives real business insight.

👥 2. Customer Management (TanStack Table)

Columns:

Name

Phone

Total visits

Points

Last visit

Status

Business can:

Manually add points

Remove points

Reset rewards

🎁 3. Reward Setup

Business defines rules like:

10 visits = Free coffee

5 visits = 20% discount

100 points = $5 voucher

They can edit anytime.

📈 4. Analytics

See:

Repeat rate

Customer growth

Peak visiting days

Average visits per customer

This helps increase revenue strategically.

💰 Why This Is Powerful

Let’s do simple math.

If a coffee shop has:

300 customers

Loyalty increases return rate by 20%

Average spend = $4

That’s thousands of dollars more per month.

Retention is cheaper than acquiring new customers.
